About Us

Our Story

We are two young students who share a passion for cooking. During our studies, we noticed that many of our fellow students had trouble finding delicious and easy recipes. So, we decided to create a website that offers easy-to-follow recipes.

Our Team

Anna Beispiel

Anna studies nutritional sciences and has a passion for healthy and balanced eating. She creates most of our recipes and ensures they are both delicious and nutritious. Besides her studies, she is involved in various nutrition projects and loves discovering new ingredients and cooking methods.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help students and amateur cooks create delicious and easy meals. We believe that cooking should be fun and uncomplicated, and we want to share our passion and knowledge with the world. In addition to our recipes, we also offer tips and tricks related to cooking and healthy eating.
