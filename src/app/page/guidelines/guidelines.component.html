Guidelines

How to Use the Website

Recipes Are Recipes

Our website offers a wide variety of recipes to suit every taste and dietary preference. Whether you're looking for a quick snack, a hearty meal, or a delectable dessert, you'll find it here.

Cookbooks Are Collections of Recipes

Cookbooks on our website are curated collections of recipes. You can find cookbooks on various themes, such as "Vegan Delights," "Quick Weeknight Dinners," or "Holiday Favorites." They provide an easy way to explore multiple recipes centered around a specific theme or occasion.

Search by Name of Recipes

You can search for recipes by entering the name of the recipe in the search bar. This is the quickest way to find a specific recipe if you know its name.

Search by Tags

Tags are keywords that help categorize recipes based on common themes or ingredients. For example, you might find tags like "vegan," "gluten-free," or "quick and easy." Searching by tags allows you to find recipes that fit specific criteria.

Search by Subtags

Subtags are more specific categories within a main tag. For example, under the main tag "vegan," you might find subtags like "vegan desserts" or "vegan main courses." Searching by subtags helps narrow down your search even further.

Search by Ingredients

If you have specific ingredients on hand and want to find recipes that use them, you can search by ingredients. Simply enter the ingredients you have, and our website will suggest recipes that include those items.

Search Cookbooks

You can also search for entire cookbooks by name or theme. This is a great way to find a collection of recipes that match your interests or dietary needs.

How to Submit Recipes

We welcome contributions from our community! To submit a recipe, click on the "Submit Recipe" button on the homepage. Fill out the submission form with the recipe details, including ingredients, instructions, and any relevant tags. Once submitted, our team will review and publish it on the website.

How to Create Cookbooks

Creating a cookbook is easy! Once you've submitted a few recipes, you can compile them into a cookbook. Go to your profile page, click on "Create Cookbook," and select the recipes you want to include. Give your cookbook a title and a description, and you're all set!

Profile Guidelines

Creating Your Profile

Start by signing up and creating your profile. Provide some basic information about yourself, including your name, email address, and a profile picture.

Editing Your Profile

You can edit your profile at any time by going to your profile page and clicking on "Edit Profile." Update your personal information, profile picture, and bio to keep your profile up-to-date.

Managing Your Recipes

Your profile page allows you to manage all the recipes you've submitted. You can edit, delete, or view the status of each recipe from your profile.

Creating and Managing Cookbooks

From your profile, you can create new cookbooks and manage existing ones. Add or remove recipes, update the cookbook title and description, and share your cookbooks with others.

Interacting with the Community

Engage with other members of our community by commenting on recipes, liking your favorites, and following other users whose recipes you enjoy. Building connections and sharing feedback helps make our community vibrant and supportive.
